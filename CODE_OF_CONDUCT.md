# Contributor Covenant 3.1 Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make our community welcoming, safe, open and equitable for all to maintain a diverse, inclusive, and healthy space.

We are committed to fostering an environment that respects and promotes the dignity, rights, and contributions of all individuals, as well as prohibits harassment and discrimination so people can contribute to their heart's fullest content.

There are no exceptions made for characteristics including race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical characteristics (including body size and personal appearance), neurodiversity, visible or invisible disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socio-economic position, level of experience or education, or any other status.

The same privileges of participation are extended to everyone who participates in good faith and in accordance with this Covenant.

## Encouraged Behaviors

While acknowledging differences in social norms, we all strive to meet our community's expectations for positive behavior. We also understand that our words and actions may be interpreted differently than we intend based on culture, background, or native language.

With these considerations in mind, we agree to behave mindfully toward each other and act in ways that center our shared values, including:

1.  Respecting the **purpose of our community**, our activities, and our ways of gathering.
2.  Engaging **kindly and honestly** with others.
3.  Practicing delicacy (defined as emotional sensitivity and care) toward others.
    - Act with **empathy, sympathy, and careful words and actions** to help people feel better and reduce their loneliness.
    - Step back to make **room** for **underrepresented and unheard people.**
    - Practicing patience, reassuring, and guiding people to **express themselves fully.**
    - Being **collaborative**, working together to achieve common goals and resolve disagreements constructively, clearly, and trustworthily. Try to understand why conflicts happen.
    - Being a **good listener** who knows exactly when to listen instead of speak.
    - Giving **specific, useful advice** at the right moment. Do not try to be controlling and bossy.
4.  Respecting **different viewpoints** and experiences.
5.  **Taking responsibility** for our actions and contributions. Apologizing genuinely to people affected by our mistakes.
6.  Gracefully giving and accepting **constructive feedback**.
7.  Committing to **repairing harm** when it occurs.
8.  Focusing more on what's **beneficial** for the **whole community**, not just personal interests.
9.  Complying with all **applicable laws and regulations**, as well as any specific policies of the platform or event you are participating in.
10. Using **clear and inclusive language**, especially in documentation, discussions, or community updates. Embracing diversity and promoting an inclusive atmosphere.
11. Behaving in other ways that promote and sustain the **well-being of our community**.

### Why People should be Compassionate with One Another

Beyond the earlier considerations, we recognize that people are not machines or objects, but human beings with consciousness, lived experiences, emotions, feelings, and personal limits. So why should anyone be allowed to act with cruelty or toxicity toward others, while expecting to be treated with respect themselves?

We also understand that while the endurance and patience of individuals may vary, some people are sensitive, and others are resilient; we should not attempt in any way to test their boundaries, tease them, or push them to their limits. Instead, we must be kind to others and behave with thoughtfulness and care.

Overall, every person deserves to be empathized with, respected, treated equally, have equal opportunities, and receive equitable access to resources and support provided by the community.

Additionally, please do not use these behaviors as a mask to manipulate and gaslight others into behaving unacceptably or serve selfish purposes in any way.

## Restricted Behaviors

We agree to restrict the following behaviors in our community. Instances, threats, and promotion of these behaviors are violations of this Code of Conduct.

1.  **Harassment.** Violating explicitly expressed boundaries, bullying, humiliating people, or engaging in unnecessary personal attention after any clear request to stop.
2.  **Character attacks.** Making insulting, demeaning, or pejorative comments directed at a community member or group of people.
3.  **Stereotyping or discrimination.** Characterizing, prejudicing, and biasing anyone’s personality or behavior on the basis of immutable identities or traits, especially disability and medical condition.
4.  **Sexualization.** Behaving in a way that would generally be considered inappropriately intimate in the context or purpose of the community. This includes using sexualized language, expressions, visuals, attention, or advances of any kind.
5.  **Violating confidentiality.** Sharing or acting on someone's personal or private information without their permission.
6.  **Misinformation.** Creating or spreading false or misleading information that damages trust, causes confusion, or incites conflict within the community.
7.  **Finding Loopholes.** Manipulating community norms to excuse harmful behavior, silence others, or to avoid responsibility.
8.  **Being Disruptive.** Dominating discussions, interrupting others, trolling, intimidating, or stalking, especially in group or collaborative settings.
9.  **Endangerment.** Causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or other harm toward any person or group.
10. **Retaliation.** Retaliating against people who speak up about harm and intolerable behavior in good faith, whether directly or indirectly.
11. Behaving in other ways that **threaten the well-being** of our community.

### Other Restrictions

1.  **Misleading identity.** Impersonating someone else for any reason, or pretending to be someone else to evade enforcement actions. That is being irresponsible.
2.  **Failing to credit sources.** Not properly crediting the sources of content you contribute.  
    Everyone deserves recognition for putting effort into making such works. Moreover, not giving credit can lead to legal trouble, and uncredited work can be seen as suspicious.
3.  **Promotional materials.** Sharing marketing or other commercial content in a way that is outside the norms of the community.  
    We strive to make our spaces focused, on-topic, clean and organized. **That** is the norms of our community.  
    Examples of promotional materials that are outside the norms of our community includes: unsolicited advertisements and sponsorships, spamming marketing links, excessive and off-topic self-promotion, or posting content that is irrelevant to or disrupts discussions, advertising in inappropriate channels, or promoting marketing content in any way that irritates or intimidates community members.
4.  **Irresponsible communication.** Failing to responsibly present content which includes, links or describes any other restricted behaviors.  
    Content that discusses, links to, or references any restricted or harmful behavior must be handled with care and context.  
    Sharing such material in ways that normalize, glorify, mock, or trivialize serious issues is not acceptable. If you're discussing these topics for educational, reporting, or reflective purposes, provide clear context and use appropriate content warnings.

### More About the Coverage of Restricted Behaviors

The behaviors we listed here are examples of behaviors that are from discouraged to unacceptable. Please note that those behaviors are **examples** to help you visualize what behavior is considered unacceptable.

We did explicitly states that "Behaving in other ways that **threaten the well-being** of our community." is restricted, such behaviors intimidates, disrupts, or has logical and valid reasons that prove the behavior or series of behaviors negatively impact the community. We cannot list all examples of unacceptable behavior here. Otherwise, this Code of Conduct would take thousands of pages. That's why we have to leave the list open-ended.

In other words, the lists of Restricted Behaviors are **not exhaustive**. The range of Restricted Behaviors is **not bound** to the list of Restricted Behaviors above, but rather extends to every unpleasant or intolerable behavior possible.

## Enforcement Responsibilities of Community Moderators

Community Moderators are authorized and responsible for:

- Clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct, adhering to and upholding Encouraged Behaviors, and reducing and discouraging Restricted Behaviors.
- Using Community Moderators' reasonableness and perception or the guidelines in the **"Addressing and Repairing Harm"** section to take the best and necessary actions against any Restricted Behaviors demonstration.
- Removing, editing, or rejecting comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct.
- Consistently communicating reasons for moderation decisions.

Community Leaders are not just rule enforcers but are also stewards of the space. Their role is to guide, protect, and foster the standards we’ve agreed on. This means taking action thoughtfully, transparently, and with a focus on learning, not just punishment.

Still, Community Leaders are human, we cannot prevent conflicts or harmful behaviors from arising, but we can and will take action against violations.

Additionally, community leaders who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith, abuse their powers, or treat people unfairly may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the project’s leadership.

## Reporting an Issue

Tensions can occur between community members even when they are trying their best to collaborate. We believe not every conflict represents a code of conduct violation, but it is never okay to be dismissive, arrogant, disrespectful, inconsiderate, rude, or cruel. Therefore, this Code of Conduct reinforces encouraged behaviors and norms that can help avoid conflicts and minimize harm.

When an incident does occur, it is important to report it promptly. **To report a possible violation, send an email to conduct@ethicalsource.dev**

Community Moderators take reports of violations seriously and will make every effort to respond in a timely manner. They will investigate all reports of code of conduct violations, reviewing messages, logs, and recordings, or interviewing witnesses and other participants. Community Moderators will keep investigation and enforcement actions as transparent as possible while prioritizing safety and confidentiality. In order to honor these values, enforcement actions are carried out in private with the involved parties, and this includes keeping reports and the identities of victims and reporters confidential. However, communicating to the whole community may be part of a mutually agreed upon resolution.

An additional point worth noting is that if community members have the ability to resolve their own issues and respectfully discourage disrespectful behavior, then we encourage community members to do so. Expect that others in the community wish to help keep the community respectful and welcome your input in doing so.

Other than documenting incidents, community members can present concerns, whether the presentation has solutions or has no solutions, or ask questions about this Code of Conduct using the **same contact method above** which is used to report possible violations.

## Addressing and Repairing Harm

If an investigation by the Community Moderators finds that this Code of Conduct has been violated, the following enforcement ladder may be used to determine how best to repair harm, based on the incident's impact on the individuals involved and the community as a whole. Depending on the severity of a violation, lower rungs on the ladder may be skipped.

1.  ### Warning and Correction

    1.  **Event:** A violation involving a single incident, series of incidents, use of inappropriate language, or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.
    2.  **Consequence:** A private, written warning from the Community Moderators.
    3.  **Repair:** Examples of repair include a private or public written apology, acknowledgement of responsibility, and seeking clarification on expectations.

2.  ### Temporarily Limited Activities

    1.  **Event:** A repeated incidence of a violation that previously resulted in a warning, or the first incidence of a more serious violation.
    2.  **Consequence:** A private, written warning with a time-limited cooldown period designed to underscore the seriousness of the situation and give the community members involved time to process the incident. The cooldown period may be limited to particular communication channels or interactions with particular community members.
    3.  **Repair:** Examples of repair may include making an apology, using the cooldown period to reflect on actions and impact, and being thoughtful about re-entering community spaces after the period is over.

3.  ### Temporary Suspension

    1.  **Event:** A pattern of repeated violation which the Community Moderators have tried to address with warnings, or a single serious violation.
    2.  **Consequence:** A private written warning with conditions for return from suspension. In general, temporary suspensions give the person being suspended time to reflect upon their behavior and possible corrective actions.
    3.  **Repair:** Examples of repair include respecting the spirit of the suspension, meeting the specified conditions for return, and being thoughtful about how to reintegrate with the community when the suspension is lifted.

4.  ### Permanent Ban

    1.  **Event:** A pattern of repeated code of conduct violations that other steps on the ladder have failed to resolve, or a violation so serious that the Community Moderators determine there is no way to keep the community safe with this person as a member. Examples of behaviors that deserve permanent bans are committing crimes, sustained harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.
    2.  **Consequence:** Access to all community spaces, tools, and communication channels is removed. In general, permanent bans should be rarely used, should have strong reasoning behind them, and should only be resorted to if working through other remedies has failed to change the behavior.
    3.  **Repair:** There is no possible repair in cases of this severity.

These Enforcement Guidelines are designed to match our community spirit, which focuses on learning, not punishment. We give individuals multiple chances to learn, ensuring fairness and justifying escalated consequences if violators refuse to learn from their mistakes. These guidelines also demonstrate delicacy by taking mindful actions.

For instance, we correct and warn about unprofessional behavior in private. We are aware that private correction helps the accused feel more comfortable, which in turn increases the chances that they will cooperate. Public shaming can leave someone who acted inappropriately feeling unnecessarily embarrassed or judged.

We also recognize that some violations may not be intentional, and we strive to help individuals understand the impact of their actions. Our goal is to create a culture of learning and growth, where individuals can take responsibility for their actions and work towards repairing any harm caused. Community Moderators may and should provide additional guidance and examples on how to repair harm if violations take place.

Still, despite all of the reasons above, this enforcement ladder is intended as a guideline. It does not limit the ability of Community Managers to use their discretion and judgment, in keeping with the best interests of our community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public or other spaces. Examples of representing our community include using an official email address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.
